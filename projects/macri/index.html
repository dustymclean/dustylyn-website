The Mississippi Applied Cannabis Research Institute is an early-stage research initiative I am developing around cannabinoid science, education, research infrastructure and institutional collaboration.

My present work involves preparing research aims, understanding scientific and regulatory requirements, reaching out to academic partners and evaluating appropriate funding pathways.

This is not an established clinical program, a licensed cannabis cultivation operation or a claim of proven therapeutic results. Any regulated research would require the applicable collaborators, approvals, registrations and resources.

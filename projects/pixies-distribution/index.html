Pixie’s Distribution is a business-to-business extension of my retail experience. The aim is to make wholesale sourcing more thoughtful, accessible and personal for licensed retailers and emerging product brands.

Its Grand Emporium identity takes a deliberately editorial approach to a very practical business: catalog curation, product information, order guides, brand discovery and relationship-building.

The current platform describes a $150 opening and reorder minimum, wholesale access for approved licensed retailers, and supplier-direct fulfillment options. Availability and commercial terms need confirmation before purchase.

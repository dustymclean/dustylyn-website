Black Box Fields is a separate venture focused on mycology, practical agriculture, controlled-environment growing and field technology. It combines my interest in hands-on experimentation with the systems-thinking I bring to technical projects.

Among the concepts under exploration are self-contained growing environments, educational demonstrations, mushrooms, environmental monitoring and technology-assisted field operations.

The venture is in development. Proposed builds, research pathways and future production goals should not be confused with completed facilities, licensed studies or verified harvests.

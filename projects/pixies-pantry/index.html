The boutique built on transparency.

Independent retailStorefront & merchandisingProduct educationBrand partnerships

Pixie’s Pantry is my consumer-facing boutique, where I bring together independent brands, thoughtfully selected products, clear explanations and actual customer support. It is a business I founded, and it is also where I test many of my ideas about e-commerce, merchandising and education.

From building the storefront and writing product pages to coordinating suppliers, planning mystery boxes and figuring out shipping, I manage the unglamorous details as well as the creative ones.

The work spans boutique shopping, glass and accessories, educational product resources, gifts and curated collections for adult customers. My standard is simple: I would rather explain the limitations of a product than overpromise.

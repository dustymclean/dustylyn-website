01 / THE REAL INTRODUCTION

More than a résumé.
And definitely not a straight line.

I grew up in Mississippi, and I'm still building here. My background is in IT systems, communications, websites, and solving problems when the instructions are missing.

I've worked in technology, online retail, branding, business operations, and independent research. I've started projects from scratch, worked directly with suppliers, taught myself unfamiliar systems, and learned that ambition isn't the same as a finished product.

My honest pitch? I'm resourceful, intensely curious, and not afraid of complicated work. I don't have every credential or connection yet. What I do have is the willingness to learn, do the legwork, and bring the right people together.

03 / THE JOURNEY SO FAR

Not a ladder.
More like a constellation.

Each chapter taught me something I use in the next one.

THE FOUNDATION

Technology first.

Studied IT systems and communications. Built practical experience in systems, troubleshooting, web development, and making unfamiliar technology usable.

THE EARLY BUILDS

From technical work to ownership.

Co-owned Ole Brook Web Services and grew experience across digital implementation, client work, and the business side of technology.

2025

Pixie's Pantry takes shape.

2026

Wholesale, research, and agriculture.

04 / HOW I SEE THE WORLD

I'm not interested in looking impressive.
I'm interested in doing something useful.

I believe access matters. Knowledge should be shareable. Smaller businesses deserve great technology. Big ideas deserve honest scrutiny, especially when science, money, or people's health is involved.

I'm not claiming to know everything. I ask questions, chase down primary sources, admit when something is unfinished, and look for people who know what I don't.

Think independently. Build responsibly. Keep going. ✳

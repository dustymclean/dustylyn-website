01 / MORE THAN A PORTFOLIO

Not a collection
of logos. A life's work.

Every venture started with a question, a problem worth solving, or something I simply couldn't stop thinking about. Some are established businesses. Some are new and still taking shape. They do not share a single legal identity, and I believe in being clear about where each one stands.

What connects them is curiosity, persistence, independent thinking and a commitment to making things more useful, more welcoming and more accessible.

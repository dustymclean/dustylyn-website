Questions answered

Before you sign up

Does this include a brand-new website?

No. These packages maintain existing sites. New builds and redesigns are quoted separately.

What counts as a product update?

A straightforward change to one existing listing, using the text, prices, stock details, or pictures you provide. New complex listings and bulk edits are separate.

Can I request unlimited changes?

No. Every plan has a defined allowance. Additional work is discussed and priced before it begins.

Do you guarantee that my site will never break?

No provider can guarantee that. I make the agreed checks, flag problems, and recommend next steps. Emergency recovery can require a separate quote.

What websites can you help with?

I have experience with WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, and general business websites. I'll review your setup before confirming support.

What are the response and cancellation policies?

Business hours, response targets, billing dates, and cancellation terms are defined in the written service agreement. Priority support is not 24-hour emergency service.

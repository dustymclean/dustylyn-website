About Dusty Lyn

A winding route from Southwest Mississippi through Louisiana and beyond to a new chapter in North Mississippi.

The beginning

From Gloster to the next big idea.

I’m Dusty Lyn McLean—a Mississippi-born technologist, entrepreneur, independent researcher and lifelong learner.

I’m originally from Gloster, Mississippi. I graduated from Jewel M. Sumner High School in Kentwood, Louisiana, spent time living in different places around the Southwest and eventually made North Mississippi home.

My career isn’t one clean line. I’ve worked in IT, website development, commerce, technical operations, creator work and the early stages of research-oriented ventures. I co-owned a web-services business and sold it in 2026. Today my focus includes independent retail and wholesale, research planning, practical agriculture, and the digital systems that support them.

One lesson keeps repeating: being resourceful matters, but so does being honest about what you have not done yet. I’m building, learning, and asking better questions in public.

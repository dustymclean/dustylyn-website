03 / Questions answered

Before we begin.

What types of projects do you take?

Website development, online stores, technical SEO, digital systems, sourcing research, documentation and project planning.

Do you work outside Mississippi?

Remote digital projects elsewhere in the United States may be considered depending on scope.

Is a new website included in monthly support?

No. New builds, redesigns and major integrations are quoted separately from monthly website care.

How much does a project cost?

Custom work is quoted after discovery and agreement on scope. Monthly care prices are published on the Services page.

Can I apply without technical experience?

Yes. Explain what is not working and what outcome you would like, in everyday language.

Will you guarantee search rankings or revenue?

No. Deliverables and scope can be agreed, but search visibility and revenue depend on external factors.

What happens when I apply?

I review your inquiry, may ask follow-up questions and assess fit before any proposal or agreement.

Should I include passwords or financial records?

No. Do not put credentials, payment details or other sensitive information into the inquiry form.

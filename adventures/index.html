Somewhere between the road trips and the everyday magic.

I’m originally from Gloster, Mississippi, graduated from Jewel M. Sumner High School in Kentwood, Louisiana, have lived in more than one corner of the Southwest, and now call North Mississippi home. Places change how you see things—and I intend to keep looking.

I'm thankful for a life that gives me reasons to be curious, to go somewhere new, and to say yes to a little fun. These are moments from my camera roll, including getting dressed up just because.

Sometimes I want to show the world what Mississippi feels like beyond a map: the beautiful ordinary places, the community celebrations, and the freedom of making a day your own.

A personal photo and video journal, featuring moments I have chosen to share.
